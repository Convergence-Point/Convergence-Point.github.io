About

Hi, I'm Jorge. I write numerical simulations of physical systems and, when something goes wrong, I try to work out why. Most of what I've built so far is about fields in curved spacetimes, such as anti-de Sitter space.

This site collects the write-ups from those projects. Each topic has a post that tells the story, the longer technical notes with derivations and measurements, and a link to the code.
